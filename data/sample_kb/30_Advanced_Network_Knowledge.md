**DNS diagnostics**. The support analyst should establish the user,
device/service, business impact, start time, scope and exact error
before changing anything.

response, failed authentication, configuration errors, application
crashes, missing access or inability to complete a normal business
workflow.

### Initial diagnostics

1.  Verify the requester using the approved identity-verification
    process.
2.  Determine whether the issue affects one user, one device, a team,
    location or the whole service.
3.  Capture the exact error and timestamp.
4.  Check recent changes, maintenance windows and known incidents.
5.  Confirm platform and application version.
6.  Perform layered troubleshooting.
7.  Record the result of each test.

### Resolution

Use the approved procedure for the environment. Prefer the least
disruptive remediation that restores the business function. If a
permanent fix is unavailable, document the workaround and create/link a
problem or known-error record.

### Verification

Confirm the service is usable and that no unexpected side effects
remain.

### Escalation
