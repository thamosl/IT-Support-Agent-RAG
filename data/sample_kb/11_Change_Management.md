# IT Change Management

> Synthetic enterprise IT-support knowledge for RAG development.
> Validate organizational procedures before production use.

## CHG-01-01: standard change

### Context

This is a realistic enterprise Service Desk knowledge scenario involving
**standard change**. The support analyst should establish the user,
device/service, business impact, start time, scope and exact error
before changing anything.

### Symptoms

Typical symptoms include service interruption, unexpected prompts, slow
response, failed authentication, configuration errors, application
crashes, missing access or inability to complete a normal business
workflow.

### Initial diagnostics

1.  Verify the requester using the approved identity-verification
    process.
2.  Determine whether the issue affects one user, one device, a team,
    location or the whole service.
3.  Capture the exact error and timestamp.
4.  Check recent changes, maintenance windows and known incidents.
5.  Confirm platform and application version.
6.  Perform layered troubleshooting.
7.  Record the result of each test.

### Resolution

Use the approved procedure for the environment. Prefer the least
disruptive remediation that restores the business function. If a
permanent fix is unavailable, document the workaround and create/link a
problem or known-error record.

### Verification

Ask the user or service owner to reproduce the original business task.
Confirm the service is usable and that no unexpected side effects
remain.

### Escalation

Escalate when the issue requires privileged access, affects production
or many users, involves security, requires a vendor, risks data loss, or
remains unresolved after the documented procedure.

### Ticket evidence

Record user, department, asset/service, location, platform, error,
impact, urgency, diagnostics, timestamps, resolution, workaround,
assignment group and verification.
